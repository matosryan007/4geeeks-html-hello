## Hero Section
**H1:** Elena Marchetti — Paintings from Memory
**P:** Oil on canvas, built from the coastlines and quiet rooms that stay with her. Based in Portland, painting since she could hold a brush.

## About Me Section
**H2:** I paint what I remember, not what I see
**P:** I grew up on the Ligurian coast, where the light hits the water in a way I've spent twenty years trying to get right. I trained in Florence, then spent a decade painting in near-total obscurity before anyone outside my studio saw the work. These days I paint out of a converted garage in Portland — natural light, too much linseed oil in the air, and a stack of canvases that never quite empties. Every piece starts as a memory, not a photograph. I'm less interested in painting a place than painting what it felt like to be there.

## Career Section
**H2:** A brief history
**P:** Trained at the Accademia di Belle Arti in Florence before relocating to the Pacific Northwest. Spent ten years painting privately while building technical range across oil and mixed media. First solo exhibition, *Coastlines Remembered*, opened at Aldrich & Vane Gallery in 2019 and led to representation and a growing collector base across the West Coast. Work has since appeared in group shows, private collections, and two published art fair catalogs.

**Selected highlights:**
- Solo show: *Coastlines Remembered* — Aldrich & Vane Gallery, Portland, OR (2019)
- Group exhibition: *Northwest Contemporary* — Marrow Street Arts Collective, Seattle, WA (2021)
- Featured artist: Cascade Art Fair catalog (2021, 2022)
- Ongoing private commissions across the US and Italy
